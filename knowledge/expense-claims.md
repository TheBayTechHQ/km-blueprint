Submit every expense in Spendly within 30 days and attach a photo of the receipt. Claims older than 30 days are rejected.

Meals while travelling are covered up to 50 dollars per day. Anything above 500 dollars needs approval from your manager before you spend it.

Finance pays approved claims with the next salary run.
