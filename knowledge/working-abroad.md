Question from Mara: can I work from my family home in another country for a few weeks?

Answer from the People team: yes. You can work from another country for up to 30 days per calendar year. Tell your manager and payroll at least two weeks before you leave, because tax rules differ by country.

Stays longer than 30 days need written approval from the People team and Legal.
