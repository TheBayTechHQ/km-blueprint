Every full-time employee gets 20 days of paid leave per calendar year. Part-time employees get a share that matches their hours.

Ask for leave in the HR portal at least two weeks before the first day off. Your manager approves it there. For a single day, three working days of notice is enough.

You can carry up to 5 unused days into the next year. Carried days must be used before the end of March, after that they are lost.
