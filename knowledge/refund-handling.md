Ticket SUP-5140. A customer who asks within 14 days of purchase gets a full refund, no questions asked.

Between 15 and 30 days the refund is 50 percent. After 30 days there is no refund, offer store credit instead.

Any refund above 500 dollars needs approval from the support lead before you confirm it to the customer.
